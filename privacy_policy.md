Privacy Policy
Effective Date: May 19, 2026

Patches ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application ("the App") handles user data. This policy applies to all users of the App on both iOS and Android platforms.

Information We Collect and How We Use It
While we do not directly collect personal contact information (such as your name or email address) on our own servers, the App uses built-in features and third-party SDKs that collect specific data automatically. This data is used to maintain app functionality, track performance, and serve advertisements. The data collected includes:

    Device or other IDs (such as your Android Advertising ID), used for ad targeting, analytics, and fraud prevention.

    Approximate Location (derived automatically from your IP address), used to serve regionally appropriate advertisements.

    App info and performance (such as crash logs and diagnostics), used for analytics to ensure the stability of the App.

    Authentication Data: If you choose to log in using Google Play Games Services, we access your basic gaming profile data to manage your in-game progress.

Third-Party Services
The App utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or your device. We use the following services:

    Google AdMob: We use AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob uses device identifiers and approximate location data to serve personalized and non-personalized ads.

    Google Play Games Services: We use this service for user authentication, achievements, and cloud saves.

For detailed information on how Google collects, uses, and shares your data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

Data Deletion
Because the App utilizes Google Play Games Services for user login, your profile and saved data are managed securely by Google. We do not store your personal account data on our own external servers. You can manage, view, or delete your Play Games data and progress for this App at any time directly through your Android device settings or the Google Play Games app. (Note: If you do save user data to your own custom database, replace this paragraph with instructions on how users can email you to delete their account data).

Security
We take reasonable steps to protect any data processed by third-party services. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
